# Workshop and fleet maintenance software market, South Africa and Namibia (2024–2026)

Research date: 25 Nov 2026. Scope: municipalities and councils, state entities, mines, utilities, logistics fleets and private workshops in South Africa (SA) and Namibia. Source quality: "fetched" means the page or PDF was read in full. "Snippet" means the fact came from a search-result summary and the page was not opened, so treat it as lower confidence. The Namibian dollar is pegged 1:1 to the rand, so R figures read directly as N$.

## Q1. Which products do SA and Namibian public bodies and large fleets use for fleet and workshop management, and what does each cover or miss for workshop job flow and requisitions?

### Takeaway
SA public fleets mostly buy fleet management as a bundled service, not as software. They take full maintenance lease (FML), finance lease or managed maintenance from financiers and fleet houses, add telematics (tracking, driver tags, cameras), and can join National Treasury's RT46 transversal contract. The "fleet management system" in these tenders is a telematics and reporting system. It is never a workshop job-card system. In-house workshop flow, stores and requisitions sit in the ERP (SAP PM at the metros, the BCX SOLAR fleet module), in specialist enterprise asset management (EAM) software (Pragma On Key) or on paper. In Namibia, central government built its own web-based fleet system in Nov 2025.

### Cited Findings
**How public fleets are served (service bundles plus telematics)**
- Elias Motsoaledi LM tender EMLM 14/2025 (closed 1 Nov 2024; fetched): one provider for 36 months covering "full maintenance lease, finance lease, managed maintenance and fleet management systems". Every vehicle gets an anti-siphoning device, telematics, a dashboard camera, driver tags with an electronic logbook and e-speed control. The municipality has its own "municipal workshop", and FML trucks are delivered to it. — [EMLM 14/2025 tender pack, eTenders](https://www.etenders.gov.za/home/Download/?blobName=711ccfc7-fdc7-4b0e-843b-c7d0cafe7ef5.pdf&downloadedFileName=EMLM+14_2025+COMPREHENSIVE+FLEET+MANAGEMENT+SOLUTION.pdf)
- In the same tender, the managed-maintenance terms of reference make the *provider* responsible for the core workshop-admin loop (fetched). The provider procures and supplies quotations, scrutinises third-party quotes and invoices, authorises repairs within EMLM's approval levels, issues an authorisation number to the repairer and keeps per-vehicle maintenance records (date, description, repairer, odometer, invoice, cost). It invoices actual cost plus mark-up and must keep vehicles available for 85% of operating hours. — [EMLM 14/2025](https://www.etenders.gov.za/home/Download/?blobName=711ccfc7-fdc7-4b0e-843b-c7d0cafe7ef5.pdf&downloadedFileName=EMLM+14_2025+COMPREHENSIVE+FLEET+MANAGEMENT+SOLUTION.pdf)

### Inferences
- In SA public tenders, "fleet management system" means telematics plus reports. The workshop-admin loop (quotes, authorisation, per-vehicle maintenance history) is usually bought as a *service* from a managed-maintenance provider. It is not bought as software the municipality runs. MOTION should not present itself as a rival to MiX, Cartrack or the RT46 houses. It is the workshop layer for bodies that run their own workshops (EMLM, Windhoek, metros), sitting next to telematics and the ERP.
- None of the telematics products reviewed shows reception → check bay → workshops → QC routing, stores issues or purchase requisitions with competing quotations. Those features appear only in ERP or EAM (SAP PM, SOLAR, On Key), which are heavy and costly. That gap is MOTION's position, but evidence on what municipal in-house workshops actually use day to day is thin (see Gaps).
- Namibian central government (about 2,700 vehicles) has just built its own system, so it is unlikely to be a near-term buyer. Town and regional councils, the Windhoek municipal garage and state-owned enterprises remain open.

### Gaps
- I found no source naming the workshop software used inside Windhoek's Municipal Garage, Walvis Bay, Swakopmund, NamPower, NamWater, TransNamib or the Roads Authority. NamPower was not found in any fleet-system tender.
- The SOLAR fleet module's feature list could not be read (BCX returned 403). Whether it supports job cards, routing or requisitions is unverified.
- AutoData was not researched. It is a repair-data and technical-information product, not workshop management.
- Mine-specific fleet maintenance stacks (Komatsu and Cat dealer systems, SAP PM at Anglo or Sibanye, IBM Maximo) were not verified for 2024–2026.

## Q2. Published pricing and price bands (per workshop per month, per vehicle per month), and typical municipal tender sizes 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Local SA job-card tools publish R149–R999 a month. Workshop Software now publishes **ZAR** prices of **R1,400 / R1,700 / R2,900 a month** billed monthly, or R1,100 / R1,400 / R2,400 billed yearly, effective 1 Dec 2025. Telematics runs at about R162 per vehicle per month (Cartrack SA ARPU). Few municipal tenders publish a rand value. Above R50m, the 90/10 preference system applies.

### Inferences
- **Correction to MOTION's recorded market read.** Workshop Software is no longer only a USD-priced product at about N$3,100–5,500 a month. In SA it lists at R1,400–2,900 a month, or R1,100–2,400 on annual billing. MOTION's N$1,200 entry and N$2,400 mid tiers therefore sit *at* the benchmark's price, not well below it. Annual-billed Silver (R1,100) is cheaper than MOTION's entry tier. Website copy should not claim a large price advantage over Workshop Software.
- Lightweight SA job-card tools at R149–R600 anchor the low end. A 5–10 bay buyer comparing on price alone will see MOTION as mid-market. The differentiators have to be council-grade features (routing, QC, requisitions with quotes, clocking, offline floor app) and Namibian VAT and NAD handling.
- Municipal fleet money mostly goes on lease and telematics bundles worth tens of millions. A standalone workshop-software line item would be small by comparison: a "quoted per site" annual licence fits under quotation thresholds or as a sub-component.

### Gaps
- No SA or Namibian vendor was found publishing per-vehicle-per-month *fleet maintenance* (not telematics) pricing.
- Awarded rand values for 2025–2026 municipal fleet-system tenders were not found. eTenders award notices would need to be checked one by one.
- MiX and Webfleet SA list prices are not published.
- Local job-card tool prices are from search snippets, not fetched pages.

## Q3. Notable 2024–2026 tenders and procurements (SA metros, district and local municipalities, Namibian councils, state entities)

### Takeaway
SA has a steady flow of 36-month municipal tenders for "fleet management system", tracking or bundled FML, and the RT46-2026 transversal contract went live on 1 Jul 2026 with municipal participants. In Namibia, central government built its own system rather than tendering. The visible Namibian notices are maintenance, lease and fleet-management *services* (Roads Authority, Ministry of Environment pre-qualification), not software.

- **Unnamed buyer:** "supply, delivery, installation and commissioning of a fleet tracking management system", deadline 18 Sep 2026 (snippet; aggregator). — [GlobalTenders](https://www.globaltenders.com/rfp/fleet-management-tenders)
- **Kharas Regional Council:** mass distance charges for its own heavy-vehicle fleet (2025; not a system). — [NamibiaTenders](https://www.namibiatenders.com/tender/provision-for-mass-distance-charges-heavy-vehicles-own-fleet-6210b39.php)

### Inferences
- The SA municipal procurement pattern is 36-month, SCM-run, functionality-gated (70-point thresholds), preference-point bids, usually bundled with tracking and leasing. A software-only vendor would struggle to bid as prime on these. It fits better as a subcontractor (EMLM caps subcontracting at 30%, to local SMMEs), as a quotation-threshold purchase, or by selling to fleet-management houses and their merchant garages.
- RT46-2026 Category A requires data integration. Any workshop system used by an RT46 participant municipality would likely need to export job, cost and odometer data into Afrirent's platform.
- Under Afrirent's RT46 merchant network (7,960 garages), private workshops doing state repair work must handle merchant codes, inspection-driven onboarding and CSD registration. Quote-and-authorisation records are a concrete pain point MOTION can speak to for private workshops.

### Gaps
- No Namibian council (Windhoek, Walvis Bay, Swakopmund, Oshakati, regional councils), NamPower, NamWater or TransNamib tender for a fleet or workshop *system* was found for 2024–2026. The Central Procurement Board of Namibia's portal was not searchable through these tools.
- No SA metro (Tshwane, eThekwini, Cape Town, Nelson Mandela Bay) 2024–2026 tender for workshop-management software specifically was found.
- The RT46-2026 Category A specification (the data-integration platform's interface requirements) was not read.

## Q4. Trends: telematics integration, mobile and offline technician apps, e-invoicing, cloud vs on-premise in the public sector

### Takeaway
Telematics is the default buy, and data consolidation is now mandated nationally (RT46 Cat A). Mandatory structured e-invoicing is coming in both countries but is not yet in force: SA is targeting about 2028 on Peppol, and Namibia dropped its April 2026 date for a phased rollout over 2026/27–2028/29. Public tenders specify "web-based" systems. I found little sourced evidence on offline technician apps.

### Cited Findings
- **Telematics integration.**
  - RT46-2026 requires Category B–E providers (fuel, tracking, condition, auctions) to upload data into the Category A unified fleet reporting platform (snippet, Treasury Q&A). — [RT46-2026 Q&A](https://www.treasury.gov.za/divisions/ocpo/ostb/RT46-2026%20Questions%20and%20Answers.pdf)
  - EMLM requires a fleet management system that interfaces "with all fleet system to provide a consolidated report" (fetched). — [EMLM 14/2025](https://www.etenders.gov.za/home/Download/?blobName=711ccfc7-fdc7-4b0e-843b-c7d0cafe7ef5.pdf&downloadedFileName=EMLM+14_2025+COMPREHENSIVE+FLEET+MANAGEMENT+SOLUTION.pdf)
  - Powerfleet says AI video bookings grew more than 50% in FY2026 and is consolidating its roadmap under Unity (snippet). — [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/2468030/powerfleet-reports-results-for-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-fiscal-2026)
- **Cloud vs on-premise.**
  - EMLM specifies "web-based VMT systems" and access to a web-based application (fetched). — [EMLM 14/2025](https://www.etenders.gov.za/home/Download/?blobName=711ccfc7-fdc7-4b0e-843b-c7d0cafe7ef5.pdf&downloadedFileName=EMLM+14_2025+COMPREHENSIVE+FLEET+MANAGEMENT+SOLUTION.pdf)
  - Namibia's Ministry of Works and Transport system is web-based on government ICT portals and was built in-house (fetched). — [New Era](https://neweralive.na/?p=364654)
  - Workshop Software has no African hosting region; it runs on AWS in Australia, the US and Europe (fetched). — [Workshop Software AI info](https://workshopsoftware.com/ai-info/)
- **Mobile apps.** Workshop Software offers free native iOS and Android apps, with technician clock on and off from Gold up. Offline use is not stated (fetched). — [Workshop Software AI info](https://workshopsoftware.com/ai-info/)
- **SA e-invoicing.**
  - SARS and National Treasury confirmed on 3 Feb 2026 a Peppol five-corner model with near-real-time VAT reporting. — [KPMG TaxNewsFlash, Feb 2026](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/02/south-africa-tax-authority-confirms-multi-year-e-invoicing-digital-reporting-reform.html)
  - SARS published a VAT Modernisation Consultation Paper on 17 Aug 2026; comments closed 16 Oct 2026. — [SARS](https://www.sars.gov.za/vat-modernisation-consultation-paper-august-2026/); [KPMG, Aug 2026](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/08/tnf-south-africa-public-consultation-on-e-invoicing-and-digital-reporting.html)
  - Target date: 2028 per several advisers, but vatcalc lists 2030 for the full digital VAT system, so sources conflict (snippets). — [Fiscal Requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/5082-south-africa-formalizes-2028-target-for-mandatory-peppol-based-e-invoicing); [vatcalc](https://vatcalc.com/south-africa/south-africa-vat-digital-reporting-consultation)
- **Namibia e-invoicing.**
  - The original plan was a single launch for VAT-registered taxpayers in April 2026. The 2026/27 budget replaced it with a phased rollout over the MTEF period 2026/27–2028/29, with no phase dates (fetched, KPMG, 15 Jun 2026). — [KPMG TaxNewsFlash](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/06/tnf-namibia-mandatory-e-invoicing-implementation-timeline-revised.html)
  - The planned design connects business cash registers to NamRA's Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS) for real-time reporting, starting with B2B (snippet). — [EDICOM](https://edicomgroup.com/blog/mandatory-electronic-invoice-namibia)
  - Some commentators read 2028 as the realistic date (snippet). — [SSON](https://sharedserviceslink.com/news/namibia-s-2026-to-2027-national-budget-confirms-e-invoicing-likely-2028)

### Inferences
- MOTION's invoice and document model should be ready to emit structured invoices: Peppol for SA, and an ITAS connection or export for Namibia. Neither mandate bites before about 2028, so the website should say "built for" e-invoicing, not "compliant with" it.
- Telematics and data-integration expectations mean MOTION needs odometer and engine-hours ingestion from MiX or Cartrack, and a reporting export. It does not need its own tracking.
- An offline floor app is a real differentiator, but I found no tender or vendor evidence that buyers currently ask for it. Pitch it as a fact of patchy workshop connectivity, not as a procurement requirement.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 SA or Namibian source was found on public-sector cloud vs on-premise *preferences* for workshop systems specifically. Data-residency rules (POPIA in SA, Namibia's pending data protection law) were not researched.
- No evidence was found on how many SA or Namibian public workshops use mobile or offline technician apps today.

## Q5. Where an integrated workshop-plus-fleet product fits

### Takeaway
The vendors reviewed sit in five groups:

| Group | Examples | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Telematics | MiX, Cartrack, Webfleet | Location, driver behaviour, service reminders |
| Fleet service houses | Afrirent, Fleet Africa, Bidvest | Lease, managed maintenance, fuel, merchant networks |
| ERP and EAM | SAP PM, SOLAR, Pragma On Key | Heavy, costly, full asset and stores |
| Global workshop SaaS | Workshop Software | Private garages, ZAR-priced, no African hosting |
| Local job-card tools | Garage Manager and others, R149–R999 | Lightweight job cards |

No source showed one product covering in-house public workshop routing (reception → check bay → workshops → QC), stores plus purchase requisitions with quotations, mechanic time clocking and an offline floor app, at a per-site price, for Namibia and SA.

### Cited Findings
- Telematics maintenance stops at reminders, schedules and service history (Cartrack; snippet). — [Cartrack](https://cartrack.co.za/blog/fleet-maintenance-management-software-run-your-fleet-smarter)
- In SA municipal contracts, the managed-maintenance provider handles quotes, authorisation numbers and maintenance records as a service. The municipality still has its own workshop (EMLM; fetched). — [EMLM 14/2025](https://www.etenders.gov.za/home/Download/?blobName=711ccfc7-fdc7-4b0e-843b-c7d0cafe7ef5.pdf&downloadedFileName=EMLM+14_2025+COMPREHENSIVE+FLEET+MANAGEMENT+SOLUTION.pdf)
- The benchmark has stock and orders, supplier invoices and clocking (Gold and up), but no purchase requisitions, no stated offline mode and no African hosting (fetched). — [Workshop Software](https://workshopsoftware.com/ai-info/)
- Namibian central government built its own system, and City of Windhoek runs its own municipal garage (fetched and snippet). — [New Era](https://neweralive.na/?p=364654); [City of Windhoek](https://www.windhoekcc.org.na/municipal-fleet/)
- Over 7,960 private garages are now RT46 merchants. They must be CSD-registered and inspected, and they work on authorised jobs (fetched). — [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/business/2026-08-29-black-owned-garages-say-govt-fleet-repair-work-slowing/)

### Inferences
- **Accurate positioning for the website:** a workshop management system for organisations that run their own workshops, such as councils, utilities, mines and logistics depots, plus 5–10 bay private workshops. It complements tracking and the ERP and does not replace them. Say "works alongside your tracking provider and pushes to your financial system". Do not say "fleet management system", because in SA tenders that term means telematics.
- **Buyer segments by realism:**
  1. Namibian town and regional councils, and state-owned enterprises running in-house garages: open, but no tender evidence found.
  2. SA small and medium municipalities with in-house workshops, as a sub-component or quotation-threshold buy.
  3. Mines and logistics depots now on spreadsheets or FleetDomain-class tools.
  4. Private 5–10 bay workshops, including RT46 merchant garages, which need quote and authorisation trails.

  SA metros already have SAP PM. Namibian central government has just built its own system.
- **Pricing claims must change.** Workshop Software's ZAR prices (R1,100–2,900) overlap MOTION's N$1,200 and N$2,400 tiers. The differentiation is scope (routing, QC, requisitions with quotes, offline floor app, Namibian VAT and NAD, local hosting and support), not price.

### Gaps
- No primary evidence was found on what Namibian councils pay, or would pay, for workshop software. The council segment still rests on MOTION's own market read, not on published tenders.
- No competitor was verified as offering vehicle routing through reception, check bay, workshops and QC as a named workflow. Absence in the sources reviewed is not proof of absence in the market.
